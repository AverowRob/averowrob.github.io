Privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Averow assistant is a private app used only by its owner. It has no other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app reads his Google Calendar and Gmail, adds calendar events he approves, and sends emails he approves.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to show the owner his own schedule, messages and to-do items. It is stored on the owner's own computer. Access tokens are kept in the macOS Keychain.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
